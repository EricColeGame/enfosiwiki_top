Major bosses trigger behavioral shifts at predetermined health thresholds—typically at seventy-five, fifty, and twenty-five percent remaining health. When a boss enters a phase shift, disengage immediately. 

Bosses often unleash invulnerable shockwaves or summon reinforcements during transitions. Wait for the dust to clear, eliminate summoned minions first, and re-engage the primary target once the danger clears.
