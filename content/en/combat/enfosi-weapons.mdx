## Core Weapon Classes in Enfosi: Strengths and Playstyles

The combat system divides its armory into distinct weapon categories, each tailored to specific tactical situations, range intervals, and stat investments. Success demands matching your personal movement habits to the intrinsic recovery profiles of your chosen arms.

### Light Blades and Daggers

Light blades focus on swift consecutive strikes, minimal recovery frames, and rapid status buildup. While their single-hit damage remains modest, their ability to trigger sustained critical hits makes them lethal against lightly armored targets.

### Heavy Greatswords and Polearms

Heavy weapons command spacing through sweeping hitboxes and superior stagger potential. They require strict timing and spatial awareness, as whiffed swings leave significant vulnerability windows. However, landing a fully charged heavy attack often interrupts enemy combo strings entirely.

### Ranged Bows and Ballistas

Ranged armaments provide safety through distance, enabling continuous pressure while avoiding hazardous close-quarters hazards. Bows reward precision weak-point hits, whereas heavier ballistic weapons trade draw speed for devastating armor penetration.

### Focus Catalysts and Arcane Arms

Catalysts channel raw energy into focused projectiles, area-of-effect hazards, or protective barriers. Unlike physical blades, these weapons draw heavily from mental or spiritual pools, scaling directly with attunement and elemental proficiencies.

## Synergies, Affinities, and Stat Scaling Mechanics

Raw physical attack power only represents a fraction of your offensive potential. Advanced combat revolves around matching your weapon's damage affinity to enemy weaknesses while stacking compatible status ailments.

### Physical Affinities: Slash, Strike, and Thrust

Every physical armament inflicts one or more core physical damage types:
- **Slash:** Highly effective against unarmored or beast-type enemies; penalized heavily against solid plate armor.
- **Strike:** Crushes bone and rigid armor plates, delivering maximum stagger against fortified targets.
- **Thrust:** Capitalizes on counter-attack frames, dealing amplified puncture damage when hitting targets mid-swing.

### Elemental and Status Infusions

Imbuing armaments with elemental catalysts alters baseline scaling, converting a portion of kinetic power into magical or volatile damage.

| Damage / Affinity Type | Associated Status Effect | Optimal Target Profile | Countered By |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Corrosive / Acid** | Armor Degradation | Fortified Knight Archetypes | Alchemical Ward Shields |
| **Radiant / Solar** | Blindness & DoT | Abyssal / Undead Entities | Prismatic Resistance Gear |
| **Cryo / Frost** | Movement & Stamina Slow | Aggressive, High-Speed Bosses | Warmth Charms & Fire Buffs |
| **Volatile / Fire** | Burst Detonation | Biological & Swarm Enemies | Water / Earth Dampening |
| **Neuro / Shock** | Action Interruption | Mechanical & Armored Foes | Insulated Grounding Charms |

To optimize your damage output with top-tier **enfosi weapons**, always carry at least two alternative damage types. If an elite adversary resists your primary slashing edge, swapping to a blunt strike secondary infused with corrosive properties turns an uphill struggle into a manageable engagement.

## Advanced Combat Tactics: Animation Cancels and Spacing

Securing victory against high-tier adversaries requires more than raw numbers; it hinges on mastering the mechanical subtleties of your gear.

### Hitbox Spacing and "Sweet Spot" Mechanics
Certain polearms and two-handed weapons feature dynamic damage zones along their strike models. Striking an enemy with the razor tip of a halberd inflicts maximum damage and posture break, whereas hitting them with the wooden shaft deals reduced blunt damage. Always position yourself at optimal striking distance to consistently trigger sweet-spot multipliers.

### Animation Canceling via Dodge Steps
Heavy weapons often suffer from lingering recovery animations after completing an attack swing. By queuing a lateral dodge step precisely as the weapon's active damage frames conclude, you can bypass recovery frames and reposition safely before the enemy counters.

### Stamina Metering and Recovery Windows
Never expend the final fraction of your stamina gauge on a standard basic swing. Depleting your reserves triggers an extended exhaustion penalty, preventing emergency dodges and leaving you prone to devastating counter-hits. Retain at least fifteen percent of your total stamina pool to ensure an immediate escape route remains available.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Enfosi Weapons

### Which enfosi weapons are most forgiving for new players?
For beginners, the **Sun-Forged Spatha** combined with a sturdy medium shield offers the smoothest learning curve. It provides reliable guard absorption, modest stamina consumption, and balanced attack recovery, allowing players to learn enemy patterns without suffering immediate punishment for missed timings.

### How do stat scaling letters work on enfosi weapons?
Scaling grades determine how much bonus attack power a weapon derives from your core character attributes (Strength, Agility, Focus). Grades rank from E (minimal scaling) to S (maximum scaling). As you reinforce your gear at the forge, its scaling grades improve, dramatically amplifying damage if your character specializes in those matching attributes.

### Can I change the elemental affinity on upgraded enfosi weapons?
Yes. Most physical weapons can be reforged using specific alchemical catalysts to convert their primary damage profile to fire, frost, corrosive, or radiant affinities. However, doing so slightly reduces the weapon’s baseline physical scaling to balance out the added elemental and status damage.

### Is dual-wielding enfosi weapons better than using a two-handed weapon?
Dual-wielding excels at rapid status infliction, high hit counts, and agility-based critical strikes. Two-handed weapons, conversely, reign supreme for poise damage, single-hit stagger, and hyper-armor trades. Choose dual-wielding when facing agile, lightly protected opponents, and pivot to two-handed arms when battling heavily armored brutes that must be interrupted.
