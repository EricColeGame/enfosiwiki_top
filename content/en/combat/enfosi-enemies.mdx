## High-Threat Encounters: Elite and Boss Mechanics

Apex predators and dungeon bosses introduce unique phases that test mechanical skill and gear preparation. Unlike rank-and-file hostiles, these encounters feature multi-layered attack strings, sudden aggression increases at health milestones, and arena-wide hazards.

To survive these high-stakes clashes, observe telegraph animations carefully rather than greedily maximizing damage output.

### 1. The Opening Stance and Phase Recognition
Boss-tier enemies rarely open with their most devastating arts. They begin by probing player spacing with long-reaching thrusts, jumping stomps, or short projectile volleys. Use this opening phase to learn dodge timing and establish baseline damage output.

### 2. Transition Thresholds and Rage Windows
At roughly 60% and 25% health thresholds, major bosses trigger transitional animations, often accompanied by an explosion of energy or an invulnerable roar. During this state:
- Back away immediately to avoid proximity shockwaves.
- Switch to defensive stamina management as their attack chains become longer and faster.
- Watch for environmental shifts, such as collapsing pillars or burning ground surfaces.

### 3. Execution Windows
Once the boss's stagger bar shatters, do not rush the critical strike blindly. If your stamina is empty, pause for half a second to let it regenerate, then land your heavy critical blow before the recovery animation completes.

Early in the journey, Stalker Infiltrators and coordinated Swarm Skirmishers pose the greatest risk. New players frequently focus all their attention on front-line brutes, leaving their flanks completely exposed to quick, high-burst ambush attacks from stealth-oriented hostiles.

### How do you interrupt unblockable attacks from enfosi enemies?
Unblockable attacks are typically accompanied by a distinct visual glow or sound cue. You cannot block these attacks with shields; you must either execute a timed dodge roll through the active frames, reposition outside the strike radius, or land a heavy kinetic attack that exceeds the enemy's active poise threshold to cancel the animation.

### Do elemental affinities change during multi-phase fights against enfosi enemies?
Certain elite bosses and phase-shifting creatures alter their elemental resistances as the battle progresses. For instance, an enemy that absorbs fire during its first phase may shift to an empowered flame form in phase two, becoming virtually immune to fire while developing a critical vulnerability to cryo and frost abilities.

### What is the best strategy when swarmed by multiple enfosi enemies?
Avoid remaining stationary. Sprint toward open ground to force the pack into a single file line. Utilize wide-sweeping heavy weapons, deploy area-of-effect crowd control spells like freeze or ground-stuns, and pick off ranged casters first to prevent incoming projectile stagger.
