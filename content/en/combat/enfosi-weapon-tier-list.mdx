## Tier List Methodology: How Weapons Are Evaluated

To rank weapons effectively, we evaluate combat performance across multiple standardized metrics rather than relying purely on sheet damage. A weapon with colossal base damage may falter if its recovery animations leave you open to counterattacks during brutal boss encounters.

Our evaluation criteria center on five distinct combat pillars:

*   **Effective Damage Per Second (DPS):** Sustained output over full attack rotations, factoring in light attack chains, heavy finishers, and recovery cancel windows.
*   **Stagger and Poise Damage:** The ability to interrupt enemy windups, crack defensive guards, and force critical vulnerability states.
*   **Reach and Hitbox Consistency:** How reliably the weapon strikes elusive enemies and multiple targets without clipping into terrain.
*   **Resource Economy:** Stamina, mana, or energy consumption per swing relative to total combat impact.
*   **Scaling and Upgrade Ceiling:** How effectively the weapon transforms as you invest skill points and rare infusion materials into late-game milestones.

Rankings incorporate extensive combat testing alongside aggregated player experience and community reports from the [Steam Community discussion forums](https://steamcommunity.com) to ensure practical validity across diverse playstyles.

## S-Tier Weapons: Meta-Defining Armaments

Weapons occupying the top bracket of the **enfosi weapon tier list** offer overwhelming tactical advantages. They combine high damage floors with mechanical flexibility, letting you control the pace of any fight.

[Dread Cleaver]     --> Unstoppable Poise + Boss Staggering

```

### Eclipse Glaive (Polearm)
The Eclipse Glaive represents the gold standard of polearm combat. Its sweeping light attack sequence features deceptive forward momentum, allowing you to slice through swarms while staying out of reach of retaliatory strikes. 

Its signature heavy thrust pierces through shields and applies persistent elemental erosion. In boss scenarios, the recovery cancel on its backstep slash allows you to avoid lethal counter-attacks without burning excessive stamina.

### Dread Cleaver (Greatsword)
For brute-force builds, the Dread Cleaver outclasses almost every heavy weapon. It provides generous hyper-armor frames early in its swing animation, enabling you to absorb incoming hits without flinching while delivering massive poise damage. 

Two fully charged heavy strikes will consistently break the posture of elite foes. While its stamina consumption is substantial, the sheer damage output per opening justifies every point of stamina spent.

### Voidspire Daggers (Dual Daggers)
Players who favor frantic, mobile skirmishing consistently gravitate toward the Voidspire Daggers. These twin blades deliver unmatched hit frequency, triggering elemental procs and critical strikes rapidly. 

Their true strength lies in negligible attack recovery frames: you can chain a three-hit flurry and dash clear before an enemy swings back. For status-focused builds, these daggers dominate boss encounters through percent-health bleed application.

## A-Tier Weapons: Reliable Combat Staples

A-Tier armaments remain formidable picks throughout both early and end-game campaigns. While they lack the outright broken mechanics of S-Tier gear, their consistency and dependable output make them staples in our **enfosi weapon tier list**.

### Cinderbrand Longsword
The Cinderbrand Longsword is the pinnacle of balanced swordplay. It combines clean thrust animations with wide horizontal slashes, giving it answer tools for every situation. Its modest stat requirements make it an ideal centerpiece for hybrid builds.

### Aetherial Conduit
As a pure casting focus, the Aetherial Conduit amplifies magic scaling and reduces mana casting delays. While it offers virtually no defensive melee utility, its spell acceleration allows ranged sorceries to strike before bosses can close the distance.

### Sunken Halberd
Sharing several moveset traits with top-tier polearms, the Sunken Halberd trades raw speed for defensive reach. Its charged overhead chops crush armored enemies, though its recovery frames demand slightly stricter timing against rapid bosses.

## B-Tier and C-Tier Weapons: Situational and Early Picks

Armaments in the lower brackets are rarely outright unusable, but they require disproportionate effort or hyper-specialized gear setups to match higher-tier options. Understanding their limitations prevents wasted crafting materials.

### B-Tier: High Specialization, Narrow Payoff
The **Obsidian Warpick** hits exceptionally hard against heavily armored constructs, yet its minuscule hitbox frequently whiffs against mobile enemies. 

Similarly, the **Whispering Recurve** delivers steady ranged poison buildup, but its damage falls off sharply in tight arena boss fights where continuous repositioning is required. 

The **Gilded Rapier** rewards parry masters with devastating critical strike counters, but its strict linear hitboxes make clearing standard trash packs frustrating.

## Weapon Upgrade Paths and Resource Allocation

Upgrading equipment in Enfosi requires careful material management. Rare refinement catalysts are finite during early playthroughs, making disciplined resource investment critical when following any **enfosi weapon tier list**.

```
[Base Weapon] 
    └── Level 1-3: Standard Shards (Found in open world)
         └── Level 4-6: Refined Ingots (Elite drops / Dungeons)
              └── Level 7-9: Prismatic Cores (Boss drops / Secret vaults)

Never spread rare refinement materials across five or six side weapons. Focus your first set of high-tier materials on an S-Tier or strong A-Tier armament that covers your primary playstyle, ensuring smooth progression through mid-game difficulty spikes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the single best weapon in the Enfosi weapon tier list?
While player preference plays a role, the **Eclipse Glaive** stands out as the most versatile weapon overall. Its long range, sweeping hitboxes, and rapid attack animations allow it to handle both mob swarms and tough bosses safely.

### How often do rankings in this Enfosi weapon tier list change?
Rankings shift whenever balance updates adjust frame data, base damage, or status proc values. Our team monitors combat performance and community feedback continuously, keeping this **enfosi weapon tier list** aligned with the latest balance patches.

### Are C-Tier weapons usable for completing the main campaign?
Yes. Every weapon in the game can theoretically finish the campaign if you master enemy attack patterns. However, C-Tier weapons require significantly stricter timing, higher stamina management, and optimal gear synergies to achieve the same clear speeds as higher-ranked options.

### Should I upgrade multiple weapons simultaneously?
Early on, you can freely upgrade multiple weapons to Level +3 using standard materials. Once you reach mid-game tiers requiring Refined Ingots and Prismatic Cores, focus your resources on a single S-Tier or A-Tier weapon to avoid falling behind the difficulty curve.
