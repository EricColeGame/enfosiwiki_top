## Beginner Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

New players often encounter progression roadblocks by falling into common traps that undermine their character's structural power. Avoiding these mistakes ensures smooth sailing from your first quest to the final boss.

*   **Ignoring Mobility Skills:** Raw damage numbers look impressive on paper, but if you cannot evade boss telegraphs or relocate during dangerous elite affixes, you will spend half your playtime running back from checkpoints.
*   **Overlooking Crowd Control Breakouts:** Many elite monster modifiers feature stuns, freezes, or roots. Always retain at least one skill or active equipment relic that breaks control effects immediately.
*   **Hoarding Rare Upgrade Materials:** In Enfosi, gear transitions occur frequently during leveling. Holding upgrade currencies for a hypothetical endgame weapon often slows your mid-game clearing speed unnecessarily.
*   **Neglecting Mana and Stamina Recovery:** Running out of your primary resource mid-fight leaves your character defenseless. Prioritize items with resource-on-kill or passive regeneration until your build stabilizes.

## Advanced Combat Tactics for High-Tier Encounters

Once you enter advanced difficulty modifiers, enemies gain accelerated attack rates, protective elemental shields, and deadly ground-targeted hazards. Navigating these encounters requires precise rotation timing and spatial awareness.

### Stagger and Break Windows

Elite bosses possess poise meters beneath their health bars. By chaining heavy-impact abilities—such as the Iron Vanguard's shield slam or the Elementalist's seismic burst—you can break this bar to trigger a stagger phase. During a stagger window, incoming damage increases substantially, making it the perfect moment to unleash your longest cooldowns and consumable damage boosters.

### Chokepoint Management and Kiting

Never engage scattered packs in open arenas if you are playing lower-durability archetypes. Retreat into narrow archways or natural terrain choke points to force incoming enemies into tight clusters. This tactic dramatically amplifies the efficiency of linear piercing projectiles and ground-based area-of-effect spells.
