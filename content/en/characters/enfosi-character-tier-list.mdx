The **enfosi character tier list** evolves whenever major balance patches, new character releases, or challenging endgame modes are introduced. Adjustments to base damage scaling or boss mechanics can elevate situational specialists into higher tiers.

### Can B-Tier characters clear late-game content?
Yes. B-Tier units can successfully clear high-tier challenges, provided your team strategy, equipment builds, and tactical synergies are optimized. However, they usually require higher gear thresholds and more deliberate manual play compared to S-Tier heroes.

### Who should I prioritize during early account rerolls?
Focus on acquiring a premier S-Tier support or frontline vanguard. While damage dealers are frequently introduced and can rotate in and out of the meta, versatile utility anchors remain top-tier investments across multiple balance updates.

### What is the most common team building mistake in Enfosi?
The most frequent mistake is stacking too many pure damage dealers without sufficient defensive support or energy generation. Without a dedicated vanguard to draw fire and a support to accelerate skill rotations, frail DPS units are easily eliminated before executing their primary abilities.
