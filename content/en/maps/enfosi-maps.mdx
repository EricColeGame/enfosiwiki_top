## Frequently Asked Questions About Enfosi Maps

### Which of the enfosi maps is best suited for beginners?
Linear Dual-Lane layouts are generally the most forgiving for newcomers. Because these maps feature clear sightlines, predictable enemy paths, and minimal jungle flanking routes, newer players diving into **enfosi maps** can focus on understanding hero abilities, wave timings, and basic item progressions without getting ambushed from unseen angles.

### How does elevation impact spell accuracy and damage on high-ground maps?
In most competitive tactical environments, units stationed on low ground suffer vision penalties when targeting units on higher tiers, frequently causing missed attacks or requiring direct line-of-sight spotters. Attacking downhill from high ground provides clear vision of oncoming approaches and frequently grants range or positional advantages over advancing forces.

### What is the most common mistake players make regarding map rotations?
The most frequent mistake is "over-rotating"—abandoning an active defensive lane to chase skirmishes across the map without clearing the oncoming wave first. This leaves defensive towers vulnerable to chip damage from unchecked creeps and results in substantial losses of passive gold and experience over the course of a match.
