## Troubleshooting Badge Progression and Desync Issues

Occasionally, you might complete the exact criteria for a badge without the notification triggering. Progression desynchronization can occur during heavy server traffic or brief internet disconnects.

If your **enfosi badges** fail to register properly, use the following sequence to resolve the issue:

1.  **Force an Account Refresh:** Log completely out of the game client, wait two minutes for the cloud save to update, and log back into your session.
2.  **Re-enter the Completion Zone:** Return to the specific boss arena or landmark where the final action took place. Moving across the boundary line often forces the quest tracker to evaluate your inventory and flags.
3.  **Inspect Inventory Triggers:** For collection badges, ensure key items are stored in your primary inventory rather than secondary storage containers, as offloaded items may fail tracking checks.
4.  **Submit a Support Ticket:** If the issue persists, capture a screenshot of your cleared objective log and submit a ticket through the official support channel for manual badge validation.

Maintaining an active record of your milestone timestamps makes it straightforward for customer support teams to verify and credit missing badges to your profile.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Enfosi Badges

### Do enfosi badges carry over across seasons or character resets?
Yes, milestone and achievement badges are bound to your master account profile. Even if you start a new character, your unlocked titles, cosmetics, and passive account perks earned through **enfosi badges** remain accessible across all secondary avatars.

### What is considered the hardest badge to earn in the game?
Community consensus ranks the **Flawless Vanguard** and **Shattered Crown** emblems as the most challenging. Both require flawless combat execution, high-tier build optimization, and zero deaths against endgame bosses with unforgiving enrage mechanics.

### Can combat badges be unlocked while playing in a cooperative party?
Most combat badges can be earned in cooperative play, provided all party members remain inside the arena boundary for the duration of the fight. However, badges marked with the "Solo" tag must be completed without companions or external assistance.

### Do hidden badges count toward the 100% realm completion milestone?
No, secret emblems are categorized as bonus prestige accomplishments. Missing secret badges will not prevent you from reaching the standard 100% realm completion reward or earning global progression trophies.
